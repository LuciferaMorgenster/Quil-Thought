# Quill & Thought – Intelligent Writing Studio ✒️✨

> **A distraction-free writing environment that replaces the friction of copy-pasting back and forth between chat apps, turning AI into a true thought partner and in-situ collaborator.**

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Quill & Thought** is designed for writers, thinkers, and professionals who want to harness the power of AI without breaking their creative flow. Instead of constantly toggling between external AI tools and text editors, Quill & Thought brings contextual AI intelligence directly onto your page.

---

## ✨ Key Capabilities

### 1. 🪄 Magic Draft from Brief & Attachments
- **Context-Grounded Drafts:** Click **Magic Draft** in the top bar to generate tailored drafts grounded in reference materials.
- **Multi-Format Uploads:** Attach text files, PDFs, Markdown documents, research notes, or images.
- **Customizable Persona & Format:**
  - **Tones:** *Thoughtful & Essayistic*, *Sharp & Provocative*, *Conversational Newsletter*, *Executive & Strategic*, *Poetic*.
  - **Formats:** *Essay*, *Newsletter*, *Memo*, *Speech*.
- **Inspiration Prompts:** Pre-loaded with prompts for immediate testing and inspiration.

### 2. ⚡ Inline AI Feedback & Smart Weaving
*Eliminates the friction of copy-pasting back and forth with chat apps.*
- **In-Situ Iteration:** Select any text or hover over a paragraph and press `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` (or click **Iterate Paragraph**).
- **Natural Language Prompts:** Input direct instructions like *"Make this sound less corporate and weave in an analogy about architecture."*
- **Flexible Revision Scopes:**
  - **Selection Only:** Target specific words or phrases.
  - **Paragraph:** Contextually rewrite the selected paragraph.
  - **Smart Weave ✨:** Intelligently weaves changes across surrounding sentences for smooth cadence and natural transitions.
  - **Article:** Harmonize tone and style across the entire document.
- **Visual Diff Inspector:** Clear visual indicators displaying additions in green and deletions in red strikethrough.
- **1-Click Accept & Weave In:** Instantly apply changes with automatic snapshot checkpoints for safe, zero-risk experimentation.

### 3. 💡 Proactive Thought Partner (Margin Sparks)
As you write or pause, your AI thought partner offers active suggestions directly in the margins:
- 🌊 **Flow & Transitions:** Resolves abrupt conceptual leaps.
- ⚡ **Pacing & Cadence:** Tightens momentum and removes passive phrasing.
- 🎯 **Nuance & Counterpoint:** Suggests anticipating reader objections.
- ✍️ **Sensory Detail:** Grounds abstract claims in concrete anchors.
- 🪝 **Resonant Hooks:** Elevates opening and closing lines.
*Review margin cards and click **Weave In** to adopt suggestions with one click, or toggle the Thought Partner on/off at any time.*

### 4. 🔓 "Unblock Me" Assistant
- **Overcome Writer's Block:** Hit `Ctrl+Space` or click **Unblock** when stuck.
- **Three Narrative Angles:** Get ready-to-insert text offered through three distinct angles:
  1. *Deeper Analysis*
  2. *Vivid Vignette*
  3. *Provocative Turn*

### 5. 🧘 Distraction-Free Sanctuary
- **Zen Mode:** Fades away UI chrome for purely focused writing.
- **Typography & Themes:**
  - **Themes:** *Warm Parchment*, *Modern Clean*, *Obsidian Midnight*.
  - **Fonts:** *Crimson Pro* (Editorial Serif), *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*.
- **Version History:** Automatic checkpoints created before every AI edit, featuring visual diffs and instant rollback options.
- **Document Library:** Auto-saved to local browser storage (`localStorage`) with export options to **Markdown**, **Plain Text**, or **Formatted Copy**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Requirements

- **Storage:** LocalStorage (Privacy-focused, no cloud setup required for local drafts)
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:**
  - `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`: Open Inline AI Prompt
  - `Ctrl+Space`: Trigger "Unblock Me" Assistant

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/quill-and-thought.git
   cd quill-and-thought
   ```
2. **Install dependencies & run:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   npm run dev
   ```
3. Open your browser and start writing!
